AXYNTRO — VIOLET SPARK LOGO PACKAGE

Brand concept
The three incoming paths represent conventional business processes and systems.
They converge into a blue-to-violet A, representing agent-based AI transformation.
The violet spark represents intelligence, automation and the moment of transformation.

Included layouts
- Symbol only
- Horizontal: symbol beside Axyntro
- Stacked: symbol above Axyntro
- Full-colour, black and white variants
- Transparent PNG files
- Favicon and app-icon sizes
- Original Agentify reference artwork for design traceability

Usage
- Use the horizontal version for websites, proposals, presentations and email signatures.
- Use the stacked version for square layouts, covers and social profiles.
- Use the symbol for product navigation, app icons and favicons.
- Use the white version on dark backgrounds.
- Use the black version for one-colour printing.

Important
Do not redraw, rearrange, stretch or recolour individual parts of the violet-spark symbol.
Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least one endpoint shape.
